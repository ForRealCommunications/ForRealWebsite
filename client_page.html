QUT School of Communication

For Real was approached by the School of Communication at the Queensland University of Technology to create a social media presence meant to reach out to current and emerging communications students and create a sense of community in the communications cohort.

For Real recieved the brief, with thirteen weeks to launch a social media presence for the School of Communications. In that time For Real developed a logo, launched social media accounts on instagram, tiktok, and facebook, and collaborated with first year coordinators to run the Procrastination event to reach out to students directly.

Social Media

For Real created the School of Communications social media to be fun and engaging to students, using our unique perspective to reach out to students on their level.
