QUT School of Communication

For Real was approached by the School of Communication at the Queensland University of Technology to create a social media presence meant to reach out to current and emerging communications students and create a sense of community in the communications cohort.
For Real recieved the brief, with thirteen weeks to launch a social media presence for the School of Communications. In that time For Real developed a logo, launched social media accounts on instagram, tiktok, and facebook, and collaborated with first year coordinators to run the Procrastination event to reach out to students directly.

Social Media

South Bank Corperation

South Bank Corporation was our first external client. They reached out wanting a campaign strategy which can increase visitation from 18 - 25 year olds. As our team was part of this target demographic, it was simple for us to create a strategy to increase platform interactions and followers with this age group.

See You at Southbank

Our campaign is "See You at Southbank", both a call to action and an expectation for all young adults to visit Southbank as their new "third space". We reframed South Bank from being the family and tourist destination to a place of rediscovery, routine and social gathering.
The core strategy was authenitic, vlogging-style reels and stories based around 4 main archetypes: the young professional, the fitness fan, the student and the artsy one. Each archetype focused on a certain part of South Bank to expose sections of the location many young adults either forgot about or wasn't aware of, sparking their curiousity and interest and framing our South Bank as the social hangout destination.
